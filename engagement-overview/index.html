Engagement Overview

Aviation Emissions & Exposure Modelling, Subcontract Support

Data, modelling and plain-language materials on EU ETS, UK ETS, Swiss ETS, CORSIA and CSRD, built to sit underneath an advisory firm, lessor, or consultancy's own client relationship and regulatory judgement.

What this is

Analytical and modelling support for firms who need the underlying data and materials on aviation emissions policy, without diverting their own senior time to build it in-house: fleet and route-level exposure modelling, plain-language briefing materials, and sourced, dated tracking of what's changing across the five schemes. This isn't compliance strategy or legal advice, and it isn't delivered as independent expert judgement to your end client - it's the modelling, sourcing, and materials that sit underneath your own team's advice, for your review and sign-off before anything reaches a client.

Working basis

Offered specifically as subcontracted support under advisory firms, lessors, airlines, or consultancies who hold the client relationship and regulatory judgement themselves. Work can be delivered under your firm's name or clearly attributed, whichever fits. Standard confidentiality terms apply; happy to sign an NDA before any client-specific detail is shared. All figures and regulatory detail clearly sourced and dated - the modelling and materials are yours to apply judgement to, not independent advice delivered to your end client.

Where this comes from

Built on real technical grounding, not just summary
Includes original, certified-data analysis of aviation mechanical and credit events - most recently the GTF grounding crisis and the SMBC/JSA fleet exposure case (44 aircraft, ~$173.5M annual re-placement value) - alongside an interactive, uncertainty-aware breakdown of radiative forcing by pollutant, a route-level carbon cost calculator (any two airports worldwide, any aircraft type, current EU ETS/UK ETS/Swiss ETS/CORSIA pricing), and a live reference on what each scheme actually requires and costs, most recently updated to reflect the European Commission's 17 July 2026 ETS review and the proposed non-EU scope extension.

Typical scope per engagement

Modelling & dataRoute or fleet-level exposure modelling, fuel burn, CO2 and carbon cost under EU ETS/UK ETS/Swiss ETS/CORSIA, built to your spec and handed off as working data for your team to apply judgement to
MaterialsPlain-language briefing materials, training decks, or reference content on ETS/CORSIA/CSRD mechanisms, thresholds, and cost drivers, drafted for your review and sign-off before anything reaches a client
TrackingSourced, dated updates on regulatory changes across EU ETS, UK ETS, Swiss ETS, CORSIA and CSRD - what changed and when, raw and factual, for your team to interpret and apply
OutputWorking files, briefing documents, spreadsheets, or a hosted reference tool, depending on what fits your existing workflow
CadenceOne-off project, or ongoing support on a retainer or regular cycle (monthly/quarterly), depending on scope agreed
