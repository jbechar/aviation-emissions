Engagement Brochure

Aviation Emissions & Exposure Modelling

Data, modelling and plain-language materials on EU ETS, UK ETS, Swiss ETS, CORSIA and CSRD — delivered as a bounded exposure report for a specific fleet or transaction, or as ongoing subcontract support under your own client relationship.

Where This Comes From

Built on real technical grounding, not just summary. This isn’t a generic policy overview — it’s grounded in original, certified-data analysis of real aviation mechanical and credit events.

  • The SMBC/JSA fleet exposure case: 44 aircraft, ~$173.5M annual re-placement value, built from certified engine data on the P&W GTF grounding
  • An interactive, uncertainty-aware breakdown of radiative forcing by pollutant — not a single-point estimate, the actual confidence range
  • A route-level carbon cost calculator covering any two airports worldwide, any aircraft type, current EU ETS/UK ETS/Swiss ETS/CORSIA pricing
  • A live policy reference, most recently updated to reflect the European Commission’s 17 July 2026 ETS review and the proposed non-EU scope extension
  • A CORSIA offsetting calculator whose core formula has been checked against ICAO’s own published 2024 figures to 7 decimal places

The Outcome

A fixed-scope answer to the question that actually costs money: what does EU ETS, UK ETS, Swiss ETS and CORSIA exposure actually come to for this fleet, this route set, or this transaction — in a document your team or committee can act on directly, with the scope and fee agreed before any work starts. No open-ended hours on either side.

The Core Offering

Two ways to work together:

Exposure Report

A fixed-scope, fixed-fee report on what EU ETS, UK ETS, Swiss ETS and CORSIA actually cost a specific fleet or transaction. Best for a one-off decision: a lease, an acquisition, a counterparty assessment.

Subcontract Support

Ongoing or project-based modelling, materials, and tracking delivered under your firm’s name or clearly attributed, sitting underneath your own client relationship and regulatory judgement. Best as a recurring input, not a one-off.

Working Basis

Offered specifically as subcontracted support under advisory firms, lessors, airlines, or consultancies who hold the client relationship and regulatory judgement themselves. Standard confidentiality terms apply; happy to sign an NDA before any client-specific detail is shared. All figures and regulatory detail are clearly sourced and dated — this is modelling and materials for your team’s judgement, not independent advice delivered to your end client.
