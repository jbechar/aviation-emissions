What carbon rules cost a fleet, a route or a deal

I cover the EU, UK and Swiss carbon markets, CORSIA and CSRD. I can write one report on a fleet or a deal. Or I can work in the background for your firm, on your clients, over time.

What I have already built

I am doing a PhD on aircraft emissions. Everything here uses real data from official engine tests and real events in the industry. Here is some of it:

  • A sample brief on SMBC and JSA: 44 planes, about $173.5M a year to re-lease, using official engine data from the Pratt & Whitney GTF grounding
  • A chart of how each type of pollution from planes warms the planet, and how sure scientists are about each one
  • A tool that shows the carbon cost of a flight between any two airports in the world, for lots of plane types, at today’s prices
  • A guide to the rules, updated for the EU’s 17 July 2026 changes and its plan to cover more flights
  • A CORSIA calculator that matches ICAO’s own 2024 numbers to 7 decimal places

What you get

A clear answer to one question: how much will the carbon rules cost this fleet, these routes, or this deal? You get it in a document your team can use straight away. We agree the job and the price before I start, so there are no surprise hours.

How it works

1

We agree the job

You tell me the fleet, the routes or the deal. We agree what is in and what is out, then I give you a price.

2

I do the numbers

For each route or plane: how much fuel it burns, how much CO2 that makes, and what it costs under each rule. You get the working data, so your team can check it and make the call.

3

You check it

If you need them, I write simple briefings, training slides or guides on how the rules work. You check and approve everything before your client sees it.

4

You get it how you want it

Spreadsheets, documents, or a web tool. Whatever works best for your team. Once, or on a regular basis.
