The LTO cycle, step by step

At low thrust, fuel burns less completely, so carbon monoxide (CO) and unburnt hydrocarbons (HC) per kilogram of fuel rise. At high thrust, the combustor runs hotter and nitrogen oxides (NOx) rise. Step through the landing and take-off cycle to see which pollutant each phase produces.

Emission indices are the median of all 586 engine records in the ICAO Engine Emissions Databank (v32) at each certification thrust setting; bars show the interquartile range. Fuel flows are illustrative values for a single-aisle turbofan and are used only to show relative contributions. Times in mode and thrust settings follow the ICAO LTO cycle: take-off 0.7 min at 100%, climb 2.2 min at 85%, approach 4.0 min at 30%, taxi and ground idle 26.0 min at 7%. Pollutant colours match the EGCC emissions charts used across this site.
