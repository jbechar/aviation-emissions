Policy Map: Carbon Rules for Flights

Carbon prices checked 24 July 2026: EU €80/t, UK £72/t, CORSIA $5/t

This page shows which carbon scheme covers a flight and roughly what the carbon costs. It covers the EU, UK and Swiss schemes and CORSIA.

1.Schemes by country and route

Drag the globe to turn it. Hover over a country to see its scheme. To check a flight, pick two airports and a plane on the right.

Check a route

2.Fleet total

One row per plane type. Give each a route and how many flights it does a year, and the page adds up the yearly carbon cost.

3.Method and limits

  1. Covers the EU, UK and Swiss schemes and CORSIA. Nothing else yet.
  2. CORSIA countries follow ICAO's 2026 list. It changes every year.
  3. Airports come from the OpenFlights list, about 6,000 with regular flights.
  4. Distance is the straight line between two airports. Real flights fly further.
  5. Fuel figures are rough, based on ICAO's BFFM2 method and maker data.
  6. The UK price may move a lot while the UK and EU talk about linking their schemes. Check it before using it.
  7. Country shapes are simplified, not exact borders.
  8. This is not advice. For filings or fines, talk to a compliance advisor.
