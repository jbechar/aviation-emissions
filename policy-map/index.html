Portfolio exposure builder

Add a row per aircraft type in a fleet or network, with an approximate route and annual utilization, to see aggregate carbon cost exposure across the whole portfolio rather than one route at a time.

Prototype coverage: EU ETS, UK ETS, Swiss ETS, CORSIA. Country boundaries are simplified placeholder shapes for this early version, not precise geography. Regulation text drawn from the same sourcing as the Climate Policies page. Airport locations from the OpenFlights airport database (~6,000 airports with scheduled/commercial service). Route distance is the great-circle (shortest-path) distance between the two airports; actual flown distance will be longer due to routing, airspace, and weather. Carbon prices are indicative spot rates, last checked 24 July 2026 (EU ETS ~€80/t, UK ETS ~£72/t, CORSIA ~$5/t) — UK ETS in particular is moving with EU-UK ETS linkage negotiations, so re-check before relying on it. Fuel burn figures are approximate cruise-phase values derived from published ICAO BFFM2 methodology and manufacturer data — actual costs will vary by load factor, altitude, and route profile. This does not constitute compliance advice - for filing or penalty decisions specific to a route or operator, consult a qualified compliance advisor.
